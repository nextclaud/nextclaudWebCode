About us

We're here to help businesses turn WhatsApp into their most effective customer channel — with secure data handling, transparent pricing, and tools that scale from startup to enterprise.

What we do

NextClaud is a WhatsApp-first SaaS platform built on Meta's official Cloud API. We help brands manage support, send template campaigns, and integrate messaging into their existing systems — without the complexity of building API infrastructure themselves.

  • Broadcast and bulk WhatsApp template messaging
  • A unified WhatsApp channel for your whole team
  • 24/7 automated chat assistance with chatflows & AI
  • Detailed delivery, read, and campaign reporting
  • Developer API for CRM, e-commerce, and custom apps
  • Transparent Meta pricing — no markup on message fees
